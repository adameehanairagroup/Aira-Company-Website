1. The Sovereign Guarantee

At Aira Group of Technology, founded and architected by Er. Adam Eehan, we build sovereign deep-tech systems engineered for independence. We consider personal privacy an inalienable right.

Our Core Guarantee: We do not sell, rent, broker, or monetize your personal information or email addresses to any third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.

2. Scope of Policy

This policy applies to all interactions with Aira Group of Technology, including:

  • Our official portal (https://airaaigroup.in) and model hub (https://models.airaaigroup.in).
  • Automated email updates, newsletters, and security bulletins sent from noreply@airaaigroup.in and noreply@support.airaaigroup.in.
  • Developer support correspondence via support@airaaigroup.in.
  • Public API gateways and sovereign developer tooling.

3. Information We Collect

We adhere to strict data minimization principles:

  • Email Subscriptions: Your voluntary email address and name provided when subscribing to engineering announcements.
  • Support Communications: Content of inquiries submitted to our helpdesk to facilitate technical troubleshooting.
  • Transient Technical Telemetry: Minimal connection metadata (IP address, user-agent, timestamp) logged ephemerally for DDoS protection and routing efficiency.
  • Zero Training on Private Prompts: Code audits, security payloads, and API requests processed via our AI gateway are processed in memory and are NOT retained or used to train corporate models.

4. Email Marketing & CAN-SPAM Compliance

All email campaigns originating from Aira Group adhere strictly to the CAN-SPAM Act, GDPR, and India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023:

  • Double Opt-In Verification: You only receive scheduled engineering broadcasts after verifying your subscription.
  • Automated 1-Click Unsubscribe: Every promotional or newsletter broadcast contains a visible, functional unsubscribe mechanism allowing instant opt-out.
  • Identity Transparency: Our communications distinctly declare our corporate identity, registered address, and responsive contact headers.

5. Your Rights & Data Control

You maintain complete authority over your personal information:

  • Access & Portability: Request a record of all data associated with your email identifier.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Demand immediate and irreversible deletion of your contact profile from our systems.
  • To exercise your rights, email support@airaaigroup.in with the subject line "Data Privacy Request".
