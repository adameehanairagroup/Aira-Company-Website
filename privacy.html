The Sovereign Privacy Pledge

At Aira Group of Technology, architected by Er. Adam Eehan, we build deep-tech solutions centered on independence. We firmly believe privacy is an inalienable right. We do not sell, rent, broker, or monetize your personal information or email addresses with third parties under any circumstances.

1 Scope & Applicability

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, and protection of information gathered through:

  • Our primary web portal (https://airaaigroup.in) and model showcase (https://models.airaaigroup.in).
  • Official email communications originating from noreply@airaaigroup.in and support@airaaigroup.in.
  • Our OpenAI-compatible API gateways, developer SDKs, and engineering tools.

2 Information We Collect

We practice strict data minimization. We only collect details essential to delivering sovereign engineering services:

Subscription Data

Your voluntary email address provided when subscribing to engineering bulletins, platform updates, or developer waitlists.

Support Communications

Information, error logs, or inquiries transmitted directly to our support desk to resolve technical assistance requests.

Zero Training on Private Prompts: Code audits, security payloads, and API requests processed through our sovereign gateway are executed ephemerally in volatile memory. We do not store or use your private data to train external AI models.

3 Email Marketing & Anti-Spam Compliance

All electronic communications dispatched by Aira Group of Technology strictly adhere to the CAN-SPAM Act, GDPR, and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023:

  • Double Opt-In Verification: Subscribers must explicitly confirm their subscription via an email verification workflow before receiving marketing broadcasts.
  • 1-Click Instant Unsubscribe: Every automated broadcast contains an explicit, functional unsubscribe mechanism. Requests are honored instantly.
  • Sender Transparency: Transmissions clearly identify Aira Group of Technology, our physical campus, and valid corporate contact headers.

4 Your Rights & Data Governance

You maintain uninhibited ownership of your information:

  • Right to Access: Request a full export of personal details associated with your email identifier.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): Demand the permanent deletion of your profile from our contact databases.
  • To submit a request, contact our privacy desk at support@airaaigroup.in with the subject "Data Privacy Request".
